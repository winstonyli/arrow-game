# Changelog

Versioning and what counts as a release: see "Versioning and commits" in the README. Each entry names the
`SIM_VERSION` and `REPLAY_VERSION` it ships with.

## v0.3.0 (unreleased)
`SIM_VERSION` 10, `REPLAY_VERSION` 1 (replays and share codes from `SIM_VERSION` 2 to 9 stop verifying; the file format is unchanged). Ships after v0.2.0 (the migration), so v0.2.0 stays a pure no-sim-change release.

- Weapons are levelled (5 levels) and held in up to 5 slots, the bow included. Orbit Blade moved onto the same mechanism (five levels, up to 8 blades); new arena weapons: Shockwave, Chain Lightning, Boomerang, Flame trail. Offer cards show NEW or the level step.
- Power Shot now applies to every weapon; Rapid Fire speeds up the bow, Shockwave, Chain Lightning, Boomerang, Mines, Meteor and Drone, but not Beam, Flame trail or Orbit Blade, which fire no timed shots; Multishot, Piercing, Ricochet and Homing stay bow-only and say so on their cards.
- Rooms keeps its original offer pool (weapons are arena-only).
- The slot cap is enforced, tested and now reachable: the bow plus four weapons fill all five slots.
- Every enemy hit now goes through one function, `hitEnemy` (`src/game/hit.ts`), instead of each weapon damaging enemies itself.
- Four arena-only hit modifiers, levelled to 5: Critical Hits, Knockback, Vampiric, Explosive Kills (numbers in the README). Boomerang and Orbit Blade get neither Crit nor Knockback; explosions drain once per tick from a 64-slot queue and show a ring at the blast radius. Knockback follows the hit direction, so a ricochet can push an enemy toward the player.
- Two status modifiers, Frost and Ignite (arena only, levels 1 to 5; arrows, Shockwave and Chain Lightning apply them, Boomerang, Orbit Blade and explosions do not). Frost: -12% enemy speed per level for 2 s (floor 40% at level 5). Ignite: 6 damage per second per level x `damageMult` for 3 s; a burn kill still heals and explodes. A new hit refreshes the timer, nothing stacks. Slowed enemies tint icy cyan, burning ones red-orange, in colours no enemy type or other sprite uses (both renderers). Burn is exactly 180 ticks and burn-kill blasts drain the same tick (`statusSystem` runs before `explosionSystem`). `SIM_VERSION` is 5 because Frost joins the arena offer pool, which moves the goldens' skill picks; replays from earlier sim versions are flagged stale. The determinism test now covers all six modifiers (new pinned hashes, Bun and Node agree) and asserts both statuses occur. Checked in a visible browser pane on 2026-10-04 (WebGL and `?renderer=canvas2d`, Frost 3 and Ignite 3 set from the console): real arrows set both timers, slowed enemies draw cyan, burning ones red-orange (burning wins), and both renderers agree. Not checked in a browser: Rooms offers (unit-tested), burn damage over time, the quiet-machine bench.
- Crit and Knockback now have particle cues (a white ring and sparks on a crit, grey dust at the old spot on a push). Presentation only: no sim change, goldens and the determinism hashes are unchanged by the cues. Checked in a visible browser pane on 2026-10-04 (WebGL and `?renderer=canvas2d`): both fire from real play and draw; the crit ring and sparks read clearly, the dust puff is faint. render-bench has not been run.
- Flame trail (arena only, levels 1 to 5): the player drops a fire patch every 20 px moved, in a 64-patch ring; patches tick every 0.25 s on every enemy they overlap. dps 8, 12, 16, 22, 30; patch life 2 to 4 s; radius 14 to 24 px. It applies Frost and Ignite and never crits or knocks back. Patches draw below enemies and shots at up to 35% opacity (`FIRE_ALPHA`), so a trail no longer hides what stands in it. `SIM_VERSION` is 6 because Flame trail joins the arena offer pool (the goldens' skill picks did not change); replays from earlier sim versions are flagged stale. New `HIT_TICK` hit flag and `fx.soft`: continuous damage (Flame trail and Ignite burn) no longer flashes the enemy white or counts as a hit for audio. This also fixes burning enemies flashing every tick. The determinism test now covers five weapons and six modifiers (new pinned hashes, Bun and Node agree) and asserts fire patches occur. Checked in a visible browser pane on 2026-10-04 (WebGL): patches draw below enemies and shots at the capped alpha and burn enemies. Not checked on Canvas2D in play, or benched.
- Mines (arena only, levels 1 to 5): the player drops a mine at their feet on a timer once 20 px from the last drop (interval 3.0, 2.7, 2.4, 2.1, 1.8 s). A mine arms after 0.5 s; an enemy within 30 px plus its radius detonates it, and the blast hits every enemy within the blast radius once through `hitEnemy` with Frost and Ignite (never a crit; it pushes only with Knockback). Damage 40, 55, 75, 100, 135; blast radius 60, 66, 72, 80, 90 px. A 48-slot pool, 12 s life and no blast on expiry keep the live count near 4 to 7. Mines draw below enemies (dim until armed, fading out), and the blast reuses the Explosive gold ring. `SIM_VERSION` is 7 because Mines joins the arena offer pool, which moves the goldens' skill picks; replays from earlier sim versions are flagged stale. The determinism test now covers six weapons and six modifiers (new pinned hashes, Bun and Node agree) and asserts that mines are dropped and detonate. 332 tests pass on Bun and Node; the build bundles 53 modules. Checked in a visible browser pane on 2026-10-04 (WebGL and `?renderer=canvas2d`, Mines 3 with Frost and Ignite set from the console): mines drop, draw as orange discs below enemies, and both renderers agree; a detonation with its gold ring was seen on WebGL only (none occurred in the Canvas2D sample), and the dim-to-solid arming and expiry fade were not inspected by eye. Not benched.
- Meteor (arena only, levels 1 to 5): every interval the weapon marks distinct random live enemies within 320 px of the player (1, 1, 2, 2, 3 meteors; interval 4.0, 3.6, 3.2, 2.8, 2.4 s), stored at their position at pick time. After a 0.6 s telegraph each mark takes one blast through `hitEnemy` with Frost and Ignite (never a crit; it pushes only with Knockback): damage 50, 70, 95, 125, 160; blast radius 40, 44, 48, 52, 56 px. A 12-slot pool; nothing is drawn from the rng when no enemy is in range. The telegraph ring brightens from 0.25 to 0.9 opacity and the impact reuses the Explosive gold ring, in both renderers. Meteor is the first weapon to consume `game.rng`, so owning it shifts later crit rolls (deterministic). `SIM_VERSION` is 8 because the hash layout gained the meteor state (the goldens were regenerated; their skill picks are unchanged) and the arena offer pool grew; replays from earlier sim versions are flagged stale. The determinism test now covers seven weapons and six modifiers (new pinned hashes, Bun and Node agree) and asserts that meteors are marked and land. 343 tests pass on Bun and Node; the build bundles 54 modules. Not checked in a browser or benched yet.
- Beam (arena only, levels 1 to 5): one beam from the player that targets the nearest enemy within its length. The first target snaps the heading; after that the beam turns toward the target at a capped rate along the shorter arc, so fast or switching targets slip past it. With no target in range it vanishes and its heading and tick timer hold. Every 0.25 s each enemy within the half-width plus its radius of the line takes one tick through `hitEnemy` with Frost and Ignite (quiet damage, never a crit or a push). dps 20, 30, 42, 58, 80; length 160, 190, 230, 270, 320 px; turn rate 1.2, 1.6, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 rad/s; half-width 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 px. It consumes no rng. It is drawn as up to 40 dots in the weapon colour at 0.7 opacity above enemies (WebGL) and as a line on Canvas2D. `SIM_VERSION` is 9 because the hash layout gained the beam state (`started`, `live`, `angle`, `cd`; the goldens were regenerated, and the arena golden's skill picks moved from Swift Feet and Shockwave to Regeneration and Boomerang because the offer pool grew; the rooms golden's picks are unchanged); replays from earlier sim versions are flagged stale. The determinism test now covers eight weapons and six modifiers (new pinned hashes, Bun and Node agree) and asserts that the beam takes enemy hp or kills. 353 tests pass on Bun and Node; the build bundles 55 modules. Not checked in a browser or benched yet.
- Drone (arena only, levels 1 to 5): up to three drones trail the player at fixed screen-space offsets, easing toward them at 6 per second so they lag (a newly active drone snaps to its offset). Each drone shoots the nearest enemy within range of the drone itself, not the player, once per interval, waiting with its timer at 0 when nothing is in range; first shots are staggered 0.15 s per slot. A shot is one `hitEnemy` of damage x `damageMult`: it can crit, applies Frost and Ignite, never pushes, and flashes the enemy like a bow hit. Drones 1, 1, 2, 2, 3; damage 10, 14, 16, 22, 26; interval 0.60, 0.55, 0.50, 0.45, 0.45 s; range 220, 240, 260, 280, 300 px; total dps with every drone firing 17, 25, 64, 98, 173. The weapon draws no rng itself (crit rolls happen inside `hitEnemy`). It is drawn as a dot per drone and a 0.08 s tracer of six dots (a line on Canvas2D). `SIM_VERSION` is 10 because the hash layout gained the drone state (`on`, `x`, `y`, `cd`, `tx`, `ty`, `age` for every slot; the goldens were regenerated, and the arena golden's skill picks moved from Regeneration and Boomerang to Regeneration, Flame trail and Rapid Fire because the offer pool grew; the rooms golden's picks are unchanged); replays from earlier sim versions are flagged stale. The determinism test now covers nine weapons and six modifiers (new pinned hashes, Bun and Node agree), with the Drone applied at level 4 because at level 5 the drones kill the sparse arena enemies before the beam or a meteor reaches one, and asserts that the drones kill or hurt an enemy. 369 tests pass on Bun and Node; the build bundles 56 modules. Not checked in a browser or benched yet.
- `test/modifiers-determinism.test.ts` pins the state hashes of a 60 s maxed-build run, run by both Bun and Node as a cross-engine check.
- Checked in a visible browser pane (2026-10-04, WebGL and `?renderer=canvas2d`): NEW and level-step cards, shockwave ring, chain zap, boomerang, blades and the HUD level strip draw, with no console errors. Not checked: render-bench cost, held-key play, a long run.
- Modifiers checked in a visible browser pane (2026-10-04; WebGL, plus Canvas2D for the card, HUD strip and blast rings): the modifier cards show their icons and tags, picking Crit puts it on the HUD strip, the explosion ring fires once per blast, and the console stays clean. The cue check is in the entry above.

## v0.2.0
TypeScript, Vite and Bun; no gameplay or sim change (`SIM_VERSION` 2, `REPLAY_VERSION` 1). The golden fixtures are byte-identical to v0.1.0 and still reproduce, so replays recorded on v0.1.0 stay valid.

- Every file in `src/`, `test/` and `scripts/` is strict TypeScript (`strict`, no `any` escape hatches, erasable syntax only, explicit `.ts` import specifiers); `bun run typecheck` runs `tsc --noEmit`.
- Vite serves the game (`bun run dev`, port 8000) and bundles it (`bun run build` to `dist/`, `bun run preview`); fonts are bundled from `assets/fonts/`. `scripts/serve.js` and the `start` script are gone.
- Bun installs (`bun.lock`), runs the tests (`bun test`, 231 pass) and the scripts. The tests keep `node:test` imports, so `node --test test/*.test.ts` still runs them on V8 as a cross-engine check.
- `bun run bench:render` (`scripts/render-bench.ts`, was `render-bench.mjs`) measures the `vite build` output through `vite preview` instead of its own static server; it now cleans up its Chrome profile and handles Ctrl+Break / console close.
- Benchmark tables in the README are labelled by engine: the existing sim and soak numbers are Node/V8; new Bun/JavaScriptCore runs and a new render baseline for the minified bundle are added beside them, not compared with them.
- Fix: a missing 2D canvas context now makes the WebGL renderer throw at construction, so `main.ts` falls back to Canvas2D. It used to fail on the first frame inside the rAF callback, which stopped the loop and froze the game.

## v0.1.0
`SIM_VERSION` 2, `REPLAY_VERSION` 1. First tagged release; unstable (0.x), nothing is promised to stay compatible.

- Core sim: typed-array world, fixed 60 Hz tick, spatial-grid collisions, Rooms and Arena modes, skills and level-ups.
- Renderers: WebGL2 instanced circles (default) with a Canvas2D fallback; DOM UI (title, HUD, pause, skill picker, game over) and personal bests.
- Replays and challenges: seeded runs, replay recording, ghost race, watch viewer, Challenges screen with daily and custom seeds, share codes, import, replay store.
- Gems are captured into a damped orbit around the player (`SIM_VERSION` 2).
- Movement trails: tails bend through each mover's real path (per-tick position history, drawn as a smooth curve); player and blades use the same tail.
- `scripts/render-bench.mjs --mode=background|headed|headless`: the benchmark no longer needs a focused window.
